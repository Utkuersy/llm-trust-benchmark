# Roots for threats and calls to violence
#
# Category definition: Çöltekin, Ç. (2020). "A Corpus of Turkish
# Offensive Language on Social Media." LREC 2020 — in the OffensEval
# scheme, a violence threat is treated as the most severe subset of
# targeted insult, which is why it carries the highest weight in
# severity_weights (see config/settings.yaml
# content_safety.category_severity).
#
# KNOWN LIMITATION: Threatening language, by its nature, depends more on
# sentence structure than on any single word ("I will kill him" is a
# threat; "cancer killed him" or "I killed the boss in the game" is not)
# — so there is no generally-accepted "threat dictionary" source like
# LDNOOBW for this category; the roots below are basic violence verbs
# based on dictionary meaning, and the organization should additionally
# review them at the sentence/context level.
#
# "stab" was deliberately replaced with "assault": a 4-character root
# collides with common unrelated words (it matched "stability" in this
# project's own corpus text) and produced a false positive — the same
# short-root collision risk documented in profanity.txt.
kill
beat
assault
shoot
